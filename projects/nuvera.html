Background & Challenge

Redesigning Nuvera Skincare for Growth

Nuvera Skincare is an emerging personal care and fragrance brand based in Lahore, Pakistan. The brand needed to replace its legacy AI-generated site with a robust, branded e-commerce infrastructure tailored specifically to the nuances of Pakistan's retail market.

The project entailed architecting a tailored Shopify experience using the Dawn theme framework, restructuring their 15-product catalogue into intuitive collections, integrating local cash-on-delivery logistics via PostEx, setting up tier-based delivery rates, and hardening store policies and metadata for organic search discovery.

Scope of Work

Key Implementation Highlights

[01] Theme Architecture

Custom Dawn Shopify Theme

Custom Shopify theme built on Dawn, with polished homepage, FAQ, About, and Contact pages designed for intuitive customer navigation and brand elegance.

[02] Product Catalogue

15 Products & Bundle Builder

15 products across Skincare, Bodycare, Fragrances, Deals, and a "Build Your Bundle" collection designed to boost average order value.

[03] Payment & Rates

Cash on Delivery (COD) Checkout

Cash on Delivery checkout with local delivery rates: Rs 250 standard, free over Rs 5,000 for seamless conversion without card friction.

[04] Courier Integration

PostEx Courier Fulfilment

PostEx courier app for order fulfilment and tracking, automating booking slips, order dispatch, and customer shipment tracking.

[05] Operations & Compliance

Inventory Tracking & Policies

Inventory tracking, discount codes, and refund, shipping, and contact policies ensuring transparent customer expectations and smooth operations.

[06] Infrastructure & SEO

Custom Domain, SSL & Launch SEO

Custom domain with DNS and SSL, plus SEO titles, meta descriptions, and image alt text configured across products and pages.
